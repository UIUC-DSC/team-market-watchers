Documentation for all columns of trades_raw.parquet.

IMPORTANT NOTES:
    To reduce the time for the script to collect all the wallets' actions*,
    the script capped the number of actions it looked at to 20,000 per wallet.
    Actions were downloaded from 11:00PM Central on 11/19/2026 to about 4:30AM on 11/21/2026.
    Market info was added by 3:30PM on 11/21/2026, and Blank markets were retried at about 10:00PM that night.
    The script did not run continuously.
    Because wallets were downloaded over about 30 hours, each wallet’s history ends at a slightly different time.
    The wallets come from wallets with at least 50 closed positions from wallets_50+_raw.csv.
    There are 72,412 wallets and 201,900,626 rows.
    Only the five action types listed under "type" were collected (rewards and other types are not included).
    About 1.3 million rows (0.65%) have no market info. Almost all of them are combos (61%)
    or conversions (32%); only about 0.05% of rows are ordinary actions missing market info.
    The market info columns of these rows are Blank.

*Read the documentation for the "type" column for more information.



Important terminology:
    Wallet:
        A Polymarket account, identified by its wallet address (the "wallet" column).
        Each wallet is one user (or one bot).

    Event:
        One page on Polymarket, grouping one or more related markets.
        For example, the event "Who will win the 2026 World Cup?" contains one market per team.
        An event such as "Will it rain in NYC tomorrow?" has only one market.

    Market:
        One yes/no question inside an event.
        Ex: in the event "Who will win the 2026 World Cup?":
            "Will Spain win the 2026 World Cup?" is a market
            "Will France win the 2026 World Cup?" is a market, etc.
        Each market has exactly two outcomes.

    Outcome:
        One of the two possible answers of a market, such as "Yes"/"No", "Up"/"Down",
        "Over"/"Under", or two team names. Exactly one outcome of each market wins.

    Share:
        A unit of one outcome. A share pays $1 if its outcome wins and $0 if it loses.
        Shares are bought and sold at a price between $0 and $1.
        All shares of one outcome have the same token_id.

    Position:
        All the shares a wallet holds (or held) in one outcome of one market,
        A wallet builds a position by buying shares, possibly over many trades,
        and closes it by selling or redeeming them.

    Action:
        One thing a wallet did, recorded as one row of this file.
        One of TRADE, SPLIT, MERGE, REDEEM or CONVERSION (see "type").

    Resolution (resolved):
        When the market's result is decided and each share's payout is set:
        $1 for winning shares, $0 for losing shares (rarely $0.50 each for a 50/50 result).

    Combo (parlay):
        A single bet made across several markets that wins only if every part wins.
        Marked by is_combo = True; combos have no single market, so no market info.

    Market info:
        The columns from event_id to profit_if_held, looked up separately from each market's
        record on Polymarket. When this lookup found nothing, all of these columns are Blank.

    Blank (null):
        A missing value. The file has only one kind of missing value; a viewer may
        display it as <null>, <unset>, NaN, None, or <NA> depending on the column's type.

    UTC:
        Coordinated Universal Time. All times in this file are in UTC.
        UTC is 6 hours ahead of US Central Time in winter and 5 hours ahead
        during daylight saving time (mid-March to early November).

    USDC:
        USDC (USD Coin) is a U.S. dollar-pegged stablecoin issued by Circle.
        Each token is designed to maintain a 1:1 value with the U.S. dollar and is backed by cash
        and short-term U.S. Treasury assets held at regulated financial institutions.
        In essence, it is a cryptocurrency version of the United States Dollar.
        All amounts in this file are in USDC.

    Market Maker:
        A wallet that keeps buy and sell orders open on both sides of a market,
        earning the gap between the prices rather than betting on the result.
        Market makers often SPLIT and MERGE, and usually have a ton of actions.




COLUMN EXPLANATIONS:

wallet:
    Contains the unique wallet ID. You can view that wallet's profile by looking up
    https://polymarket.com/profile/<wallet ID>

name:
    Contains the wallet's username. Blank means the wallet has no username on record.

type:
    Represents the action that a wallet took.
    Actions include:
        TRADE:
            Buying or selling shares of one outcome.
        SPLIT:
            When a wallet spends $1 to create one "Yes" share and one "No" share in a market.
            This is often done by market makers.
        MERGE:
            When a wallet merges one "Yes" share and one "No" share to receive $1.
        REDEEM:
            After a market resolves, turn shares into cash:
            $1 per winning share, $0 per losing share.
            Includes automatic redemptions.
        CONVERSION:
            In mutually exclusive events, swap "No" shares in some markets
            for "Yes" shares in the others, plus cash when more than one "No" is converted.
            Ex:
                In the event "Who will win the 2026 World Cup?",
                A wallet holding "No" on Spain could convert it into "Yes" on every other team.
            Only events where negative_risk = True have the possibility of a conversion happening.
            Conversion can only go from No to Yes, but not Yes to No.
            For a more in-depth explanation on conversion, go to the bottom of the document.

side:
    Whether the TRADE was a buy or sell, and Blank for any other type of action.
    BUY:
        buying shares of an outcome.
    SELL:
        selling shares of an outcome.

time:
    What time the action took place in the form YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM:SS in UTC.
    UTC is 6 hours ahead of US Central Time in winter and 5 hours ahead during daylight saving time.

title:
    The market's question as shown on Polymarket.
    For combo trades, it’s the questions of every market in the combined bet joined by "AND".
    Ex: "Angels vs. Red Sox AND Red Sox vs. Yankees".
    May be Blank for some rows where type = CONVERSION.

slug:
    A short text ID for the market.

event_slug:
    A short text ID for the event the market belongs to.
    You can view the event at https://polymarket.com/event/<event_slug>.
    An event groups related markets.

outcome:
    The name of the outcome that was traded or redeemed.
    Ex: "Yes"/"No", "Up"/"Down", "Over"/"Under", "Yankees", "Norway", etc.
    Blank if type = SPLIT, MERGE, or CONVERSION (and for about 0.01% of REDEEMs).
    It comes from the wallet's own action record, so it is filled in even when no market info was found.

outcome_index:
    Mostly 0 or 1 based on the market's outcome.
    If the value outcome is:
        0: "Yes",  "Up",   "Over"
        1: "No",  "Down",  "Under"
        999: the action involves both outcomes, so outcome is Blank
    A value of 0 or 1 can also correspond to a team name. Ex: "Yankees"
    There are a few rows with numbers that are not 0, 1, or 999.
    They are from the older version of Polymarket (2020-2021) where a market
    could have more than two outcomes; the index is that outcome's position in the list.
    There are 52 such rows, all REDEEMs.

condition_id:
    The unique id of the market.
    Can be used to group all actions in the same market.
    Real market IDs start with "0x".
    Some rows where type = CONVERSION have an ID that starts with "\x";
    these are not real market IDs and have no market info.

token_id:
    The unique ID of one outcome’s shares.
    Ex: the “Yes” shares of one market.
    Each market has two token IDs, one per outcome.
    Note: While the token IDs are numbers, they are stored as text due to their length.
    Keep them as text; converting them to numbers rounds them.
    Blank if type = SPLIT, MERGE, or CONVERSION, and for about 2% of REDEEMs.

price:
    The price per share in USDC, from 0 to 1.
    For TRADEs, this approximately represents the market’s implied probability at that moment:
    buying “Yes” at 0.30 means the market gave it about a 30% chance.
    For rows where type = SPLIT, price is 0.5, except for some rows from April-June 2026 that show 0.
    For rows where type = REDEEM, MERGE, or CONVERSION, price is 0; it is not applicable.

size:
    The number of shares in the action; the number bought, sold, redeemed, etc.
    For type = SPLIT or MERGE, it is the number of pairs created or cashed out:
    size = 10 means 10 "Yes" and 10 "No" shares, for $10.

usdc_size:
    The dollars (USDC) that changed hands, not counting trading fees.
    TRADE: about price * size. The wallet paid this on a BUY and received it on a SELL.
    SPLIT: dollars put in (equal to size).
    MERGE: dollars received (equal to size).
    REDEEM: dollars received, equal to size * payout_per_share. It’s 0 when losing shares are redeemed.
    CONVERSION: the cash the wallet received. It's 0 when only one "No" was converted,
    otherwise a whole multiple of size (see "More information on Conversion" at the bottom).

transaction_hash:
    The ID of the blockchain transaction that recorded the action.
    You can view it at https://polygonscan.com/tx/<hash>.
    Several rows can share one hash if one order was filled by several traders.

is_combo:
    True if the trade was part of a combo bet across several markets.
    These rows have no single market, so all of their market info columns are Blank.

history_truncated:
    True if the wallet had 20,000 or more actions, so only its most recent 20,000 were collected.
    It’s the same on every row of a wallet.

position_entry_fees_usdc:
    The total trading fees the wallet paid on ALL of its purchases of this outcome.
    It describes the wallet’s whole position in that outcome, not this one row.
    Hence, it repeats on every row with the same wallet and token_id.
    Don't add it up across rows; take it once per wallet and token_id.

position_total_cost_usdc:
    The total money the wallet spent buying this outcome, fees included.
    This is the total amount spent for the position as a whole, so it repeats on every row
    of the position. Don't add it up across rows.

event_id:
    The numeric ID of the event the market belongs to.
    Markets that share an event_id are related, so their results aren’t independent.
    This is useful for the dependence issue in z-scores, for example clustering by event.
    Blank when no market info was found: combos, most conversions, malformed IDs,
    and the old multi-outcome markets.

negative_risk:
    True if the market is part of an event where exactly one market can resolve “Yes”,
    such as “Who will win the election?” with one market per candidate.
    CONVERSIONs only happen in these events.
    Blank when no market info was found.

end_date:
    The market’s scheduled end date (YYYY-MM-DD), set when the market was created.
    The actual resolution can be earlier or later.
    Blank when no market info was found.

opposite_token_id:
    The token_id of the other outcome in the same market.
    For example, on a “Yes” row, the opposite_token_id refers to the “No” token.
    Blank if outcome_index is anything other than 0 or 1, or when no market info was found.

current_price:
    This outcome’s price when the script looked the market up.
    For resolved markets, it’s the final payout (1, 0 or 0.5).
    For markets still open, it's a snapshot from 11/21/2026 and changes after that.
    It is not the price at the time of the action; that's "price".
    Blank if outcome_index is anything other than 0 or 1, or when no market info was found.

market_resolved:
    True if the market had resolved when the script looked it up.
    False means the market was still open, or that no market info was found for it.

resolved_time:
    When the market was resolved, in UTC.
    Blank means the market was not resolved at the time the script ran, or no market info was found.

payout_per_share:
    What one share of this outcome paid at resolution:
    1 if it won, 0 if it lost, and 0.5 for markets resolved 50/50.
    Blank means the market was not resolved at the time the script ran, or no market info was found.

won:
    True if this row's outcome won, False if it lost,
    regardless of whether the wallet held the position until the market resolved.
    Blank if the market hasn't resolved, resolved 50/50, or the row isn't about one outcome.
    For a SELL row, won = True means the wallet sold shares that later won.
    Ex: If a wallet bought "Yes" for Kamala Harris winning the 2024 election for $0.20 per share
    and sold its holdings for $0.40 per share before the market resolved, won would equal False,
    even though the wallet made money on the position.

profit_if_held:
    The profit the shares would have made if held until the market resolved:
    (payout_per_share - price) * size. Excludes trading fees.
    Blank if the market is not resolved or the row is not a BUY trade.




**More information on Conversion:

Polymarket’s conversion takes “No” shares in one or more markets of a negative-risk event
and turns them into “Yes” shares in the event’s other markets, sometimes with cash too.

There’s no conversion action that goes from “Yes” to “No”.
This is because, in a negative-risk event, exactly one market resolves “Yes”.

Take the World Cup event with 4 teams, and a wallet holding 1 “No” share on Spain:
If Spain loses, which happens whenever France, Brazil or Germany wins, it pays $1.
That’s exactly what 1 “Yes” on France + 1 “Yes” on Brazil + 1 “Yes” on Germany pays,
since exactly one of those three wins.

The two holdings are worth the same, so Polymarket lets you swap the first for the second.
That swap is the conversion.

Where the cash comes from: converting “No” on 2 teams, Spain and France:
“No” on Spain + “No” on France together pay $2 if Brazil or Germany wins, and $1 if Spain or France wins.
That equals “Yes” on Brazil + “Yes” on Germany (pays $1 in the first case, $0 in the second) plus $1 in cash.

So converting “No” in k markets gives “Yes” in the remaining markets plus k − 1 dollars per share.
That’s why usdc_size is 0 for conversions of a single “No” and a whole multiple of size otherwise.
